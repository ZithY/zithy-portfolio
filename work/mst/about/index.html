    About MST

    From pressure washer sales to full-scale supplier.

    MST Pumps & Mining Supplies is a South African–based specialist in high-pressure cleaning solutions, industrial pumps, and purpose-built equipment for demanding environments.

    1. Where we started

      Sales and repairs

      Founded with a strong focus on reliability, performance, and practical engineering, MST began with pressure washer sales and repairs.

    2. Where we are

      A full-scale supplier

      Today we supply industrial cleaning systems, custom-built pressure washers, wet and dry vacuum units, and supporting components.

    3. How we work

      Designed around your needs

      We work closely with our clients to design and manufacture solutions tailored to their exact:

      • Operational requirements
      • Space constraints
      • Power availability
      • Performance needs
    4. How it's built

      Global components, local frames

      Trusted global component brands combined with locally fabricated frames and control systems deliver durable, serviceable, and cost-effective equipment.

    5. Where it works

      Built to perform

      In mining, industrial, commercial, and heavy-duty applications.

      • Mining
      • Industrial
      • Commercial
      • Heavy-duty
    6. Our commitment

      What sets us apart

      Quality workmanship, technical support, and long-term client partnerships make us a dependable solutions provider in the industrial cleaning and pumping sector.
