Questions

Quick answers

How long does a site take?

It depends on the package and how quickly I receive your content, but it typically takes around 1 to 3 business days from start to finish.

How much do I pay upfront?

Prices are fixed and once-off. A deposit of 30% to 50%, depending on your package, starts the work. Add-ons such as an extra page (R350) or a logo (R400) are priced up front, so there are no surprises.

Will I see the design before it goes live?

Yes. You approve the design before the full build starts, and nothing goes live until you are happy with it.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, your logo if you have one, a few photos and a short description of what you offer. If you do not have a logo yet, I can design one for R400.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every site is checked on phones, tablets and desktops before I hand it over.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with a Digital Business Card and move up as your business grows. Upgrading also gets you a discount, based on which package you bought before.

What happens when free support ends?

You can still message me any time. Small changes are quick to arrange, and extra pages are R350 each.

How do I get a quote?

Message me on WhatsApp or fill in the form at the bottom of this page. I will reply with ideas and a fixed quote, with no pressure and no obligation.
