Questions

How long does a site take?

It depends on the package and how quickly I receive your content. I'll give you a clear timeline in your quote before you pay anything.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, a list of your services, any reviews you'd like to show and a few photos. If you're missing something, just say and we'll sort it out.

How much is the deposit?

It depends on the package: 30% for the Digital Business Card and Starter, 40% for Business and 50% for Premium. It is paid before work starts, and the rest is due when the site is ready to go live.

What happens when free support ends?

You can still message me any time. Small changes are quick to arrange, and extra pages are R350 each.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with a Digital Business Card and move up to a bigger package as your business grows.
